[KOMPONEN]
; Reference | Library / Part         | Nilai / Catatan
;-----------|------------------------|------------------------------------
V1         | BATTERY                | 3 V (sumber input)
L1         | INDUCTOR               | 10 uH, ESR 0.03 ohm
M1         | IRLML2502 (atau MOSFET-N logic-level)
                                    | Vds 20V, Rds(on) ~45 mOhm @ Vgs=2.5V
D1         | SCHOTTKY (1N5819 / SS34)
                                    | 40 V / 1 A
Cin1       | CAP-ELEC               | 22 uF / 10 V
Cin2       | CAP                    | 100 nF
Cout1      | CAP-ELEC               | 22 uF / 50 V (X7R idealnya)
Cout2      | CAP-ELEC               | 47 uF / 35 V (polymer)
Cout3      | CAP                    | 100 nF
Rload      | RES                    | 120 ohm / 5 W
Rsnub      | RES                    | 2.2 ohm
Csnub      | CAP                    | 470 pF / 50V (C0G)
Rfb1       | RES                    | 287 k
Rfb2       | RES                    | 10 k
Dtvs       | ZENER                  | BZX84C24 atau SMAJ26A (klamp ~26 V)
U1         | 555 (atau IC PWM TL494/UC3843 untuk closed-loop)
                                    | Pakai 555 astable utk demo open-loop

; PWM Generator (untuk demo open-loop di Proteus):
;   - Mode astable 555 dengan duty 87.5% pada 500 kHz
;   - Atau gunakan blok PULSE generator (Generator Mode -> Pulse):
;       Initial=0V, Pulsed=5V, Delay=0, Rise=20n, Fall=20n,
;       Pulse Width=1.75us, Period=2us

[NET / WIRE LIST]
; Net name  : Pin-pin yang dihubungkan
; ---------------------------------------------------------------
VIN_RAW   : V1(+), Cin1(+), Cin2(1)
GND       : V1(-), Cin1(-), Cin2(2), M1(S), Cout1(-), Cout2(-),
            Cout3(2), Rload(2), Csnub(2), Rfb2(2), Dtvs(K=GND? lihat),
            U1(GND)
N_LIN     : Cin1(+) -> L1(1)               ; titik input induktor
SW        : L1(2) -> M1(D) -> D1(A) -> Rsnub(1)
NSN       : Rsnub(2) -> Csnub(1)
VOUT      : D1(K) -> Cout1(+) -> Cout2(+) -> Cout3(1) -> Rload(1)
            -> Rfb1(1) -> Dtvs(anode utk SMAJ26A bidir)
FB        : Rfb1(2) -> Rfb2(1)             ; ke pin FB controller
GATE      : U1(OUT) -> M1(G)               ; output PWM ke gate

[PENGATURAN SIMULASI]
- Analysis: Transient (Graph Mode -> ANALOGUE)
- Stop Time: 4 m
- Max Step:  50 n
- Probe:
    * Tegangan: VOUT, SW, GATE
    * Arus    : I(L1), I(D1), I(M1)
- Plot ripple di window 3 ms - 4 ms (steady state).

[VERIFIKASI HASIL YANG DIHARAPKAN]
- VOUT rata-rata    ~ 23.5 .. 24.0 V (open-loop: tergantung losses)
- IL  rata-rata     ~ 1.7 .. 1.9 A
- IL  ripple pp     ~ 0.5 .. 0.6 A
- Vds(M1) overshoot < 30 V (snubber bekerja)
- Efisiensi         ~ 78 .. 85 %

[VERSI CLOSED-LOOP DI PROTEUS]
Untuk closed-loop di Proteus, paling praktis pakai IC PWM TL494 atau
UC3843 (tersedia di library) dengan rangkaian feedback Rfb1/Rfb2 -> FB,
plus jaringan kompensasi Type-II di pin COMP. Skema persis sesuai
datasheet IC; nilai komponen kompensasi diambil dari hasil
boost_averaged_model.m (Rc, Cc1, Cc2).
